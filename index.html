Our Story

About AL Fareha

Founded in 2003, AL Fareha Educational High School has grown from a small community school to one of Karachi's most trusted institutions. We believe every child deserves quality education rooted in Islamic values and modern knowledge.

🕌

Islamic values at the core

Morning assembly with Quran recitation, Islamic studies integrated into daily curriculum.

🎓

Sindh Board affiliated

Registered with BSEK (Board of Secondary Education Karachi) for Matric examinations.

🌐

Bilingual medium

English and Urdu medium streams available from Class I onwards.

🏫

Safe, disciplined environment

CCTV monitored campus, authorised pickup system, and trained security staff.

Our Mission & Vision

Mission: To provide affordable, high-quality education that develops academic excellence, Islamic character, and civic responsibility in every student.

Vision: To be the most trusted school in Karachi, where every parent feels proud and every student feels empowered.

Admissions 2025–26

We welcome students of all backgrounds. Follow these simple steps to secure your child's place at AL Fareha.

1

Download Form

Get the admission form from school office or download below.

2

Submit Documents

B-form copy, 2 photos, previous result card, and school leaving certificate.

3

Entry Test

Short written test in Maths and English. Dates announced after form submission.

4

Confirmation

Result communicated via SMS. Fee payment to confirm seat within 5 days.
